User Guide & Office Assistant Instructions

1. Loading Tender Requirements

Click "Import requirements.json" to load your official tender specifications, or choose a preset like "Standard ICT & Workstations". The app immediately parses the tender ID, deadlines, and ordered document rules.

2. Uploading PDFs & Duplicate Detection

Drag and drop up to 30 PDF files (up to 50 MB total). The system reads real page counts and uses SHA-256 binary cryptography to detect exact duplicate documents even if their filenames differ.

3. Matching Documents & Expiry Validation

Each requirement can be matched to exactly one uploaded PDF. For documents with expiry dates, enter the expiry date. If the expiry date is before the tender deadline, the status becomes Expired (blocking). If it is on or after the deadline, it is OK.

4. Generating the Submission Package

When all blocking issues are resolved, click "Generate Package". TenderFlow creates an official English Cover Page, Table of Contents, merges all documents in order, and stamps <tender_id> | Page X of Y on every single page before downloading <tender_id>_Package.pdf.
